Heroes and forms

Each form is taken as an evolution path, at player level 30 with epic gear: alone on levels 1 and 2, and in every team of three it can be in on level 5 (see Teams). Team value is how much further its teams get than the average team, so it counts everything the form adds (curses, slows, buffs, energy), not only its own damage. As partner and as carry split that by how strong its two teammates are (the strongest and the weakest third of its teams), each against the other forms in the same spot: a multiplier (Verdict, Hex, Heresy, Rally) shines as a partner and has little to multiply as a carry. Power (0 to 100) depends on the role (set in tools/balance/roles.mjs): damage forms, half alone and half team value; semi-supports, a quarter alone and three quarters as a partner; supports, as a partner only (0 points is 50, ±20 points is 0 or 100).
