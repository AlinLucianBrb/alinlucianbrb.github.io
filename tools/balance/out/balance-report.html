Heroes and forms

Each form is taken as an evolution path, at player level 10 with rare gear: alone on levels 2 and 3, and in every team of three it can be in on level 4 (see Teams). Team value is how much further its teams get than the average team, so it counts everything the form adds (curses, slows, buffs, energy), not only its own damage. Power (0 to 100) is half how far it gets alone and half its team value (the average team is 50; ±20 points is 0 or 100). Support-only forms (grey) are judged by team value alone.
